Alert Delivery

Sound/notifications only fire for alerts that appear after you enable this — not for ones already on file. Browser notifications need permission and may not work when the file is opened directly (file://) in some browsers — sound alarm works everywhere.

Extreme Weather Thresholds

Auto mode sets the threshold to average + 1.8× variation from your own stored readings (needs at least 5 readings; falls back to the manual slider until then). Thresholds are saved automatically and re-evaluated across every stored reading.
